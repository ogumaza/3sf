3SF 1.4.0 rips games that keep their sound data in group files beside the sound archive. Mighty Gunvolt Burst is one. A piece that a game plays as several sequences now rips as one `.mini3sf`. Game mode now plays the reverb that Pokémon X starts in the field. Archive mode now plays the effects that Pokémon X, WarioWare Gold, Ocarina of Time 3D and Majora's Mask 3D put on the aux buses. Rips with group files, such pieces or effects require 3SF 1.4.0. Earlier versions refuse them.

3SF rips Nintendo 3DS sequenced music into a PSF-family format. The rips play with the game's DSP firmware on an emulated Teak DSP. 3SF comes with a ripper (`3sfrip`), a renderer (`3sfplay`), a foobar2000 component and an XMPlay plugin. The format is described in `docs/3sf.md`.

## New in 1.4.0

- **Group files.** Some games keep groups of their sound data outside the sound archive. Such a game loads each group from a group file (`.bcgrp`) in its RomFS. `3sfrip` used to skip the sequences whose data lies in such a file. Mighty Gunvolt Burst keeps all of its sound data in group files. Earlier versions could rip none of its music. `3sfrip` now finds each group file by its group's name. The search covers the game's RomFS, or the folder of a loose `.bcsar` and the folders below it. The `.3sflib` holds the parts of the group files that the rips play. `3sfrip` warns of each group file that it can't find. The sequences whose data lies in a missing group file are skipped.
- **Pieces of several sequences.** Mighty Gunvolt Burst plays each piece of its music as five sequences. Each sequence plays one channel of the NES's sound chip. `3sfrip` now rips each such piece as one `.mini3sf`. The `.mini3sf` lists the five sequences. Like the game, a player starts the five together. `3sfrip` finds such a piece by the labels of its sequences: one name followed by `_DPCM`, `_NOISE`, `_P1`, `_P2` and `_TRI`. The `title` tag holds the piece's name. `3sf_sound` lists the labels of its sequences. The length analysis measures each sequence alone. The piece gets the latest end and the longest fade. `--only` picks a piece by its name or by any of its sequences' labels.
- **Pokémon X's reverb in game mode.** In the field, Pokémon X starts a reverb on aux bus A. Some of the field's sound effects send to it. Game mode used to play those effects without the reverb. A rip doesn't load the game's field module. 3SF's driver now starts the reverb with the field's parameters before it loads the sound. The effects' rips play with the reverb. Their lengths now take in the reverb's ring. `3sfrip` renders each sound that sends to the reverb to find where the ring ends. A game-mode library made with an earlier version plays without the reverb. Rip the game again to hear it.
- **Effects in archive mode.** A game puts effects such as a reverb on the 3DS's two aux buses while it runs. The sound archive doesn't hold them. Archive mode used to leave both buses off. What a sound sent to them was lost. `3sfrip` now writes the effects of four games into their archive-mode rips: Pokémon X's reverb on bus A, WarioWare Gold's reverb on each bus, Ocarina of Time 3D's reverb on bus A and delay on bus B, and Majora's Mask 3D's delay on bus B. The player runs them as the game's sound library does. A sound's length takes in the effects' ring. `3sfrip` finds a game by its program ID. For WarioWare Gold, Ocarina of Time 3D and Majora's Mask 3D, the table holds the North American releases. A rip made with an earlier version holds no effects. Rip the game again to hear them.
- **Rips that require 1.4.0.** A set with group files, pieces or effects marks them in its archive descriptor. `docs/3sf.md` describes the new layout. 3SF 1.3.3 and earlier refuse such a set. Every other set keeps the layout of 1.3.3.
- **The libraries.** 3SF now builds on ntdlib 1.13.0, with xsflib 1.6.0.

## Features

- **Ripping.** `3sfrip` rips a decrypted 3DS game (`.3ds`, `.cci`, `.cxi` or `.cia`, or an extracted game folder) or a sound archive (`.bcsar`, with a DSP firmware file) into a `.3sflib` and a `.mini3sf` for each sound, or only the music with `--bgm`. A game's rips contain the DSP firmware that the game uses. The firmware can be in a file or in the game's code. `3sfrip` finds the group files that a game keeps outside its sound archive. A piece that a game plays as several sequences gets one `.mini3sf`. `3sfrip` plays each sound to determine its length and fade tags. It also supports drag and drop.
- **Two rip modes.** Game mode runs the game's sound code. Game-mode rips play as the game does. Game mode requires a driver profile for the game. Pokémon X (Japan) has the only one so far. Archive mode plays a sound archive from any game with 3SF's model of the SDK's sound player.
- **Effects.** Game mode plays the reverb that Pokémon X starts in the field. Archive mode plays the effects that Pokémon X, WarioWare Gold, Ocarina of Time 3D and Majora's Mask 3D put on the aux buses.
- **Rendering.** `3sfplay` renders rips to WAV or FLAC at the DSP's native 32,728 Hz.
- **foobar2000.** The component plays `.3sf` and `.mini3sf` files using their length and fade tags. It supports tag editing, ReplayGain, endless playback and quick seeking. During playback, the component renders ahead. Seeking within the buffered audio is instant.
- **XMPlay.** The plugin plays `.3sf` and `.mini3sf` files using their length and fade tags. It supports endless playback. Like the component, it renders ahead during playback.
- **Speed.** On 64-bit Windows, the DSP firmware is translated into x86-64 code during playback. The 32-bit component and the XMPlay plugin interpret the firmware. Interpreting is slower. Use the 64-bit component if your foobar2000 and its other components allow it.

## Known limitations

- Seeking ahead of the audio buffer waits for rendering to reach the target. A long seek soon after a track starts can still be slow.
- Game mode only has a profile for Pokémon X (Japan). Other games rip in archive mode. Archive mode's model comes from Pokémon X. A game with another version of the sound library may play differently from the console.
- Only sequences are ripped. Use tools such as vgmstream for BCSTM and AAC streams. Wave sounds are skipped. So are sequences whose data lies outside the sound archive and its group files, or in a group file that `3sfrip` can't find.
- Archive mode plays effects only for the games in its table. Other regions' releases of WarioWare Gold, Ocarina of Time 3D and Majora's Mask 3D get none. A loose `.bcsar` and the other games get none either. Some games use effects that the DSP runs. Archive mode doesn't play those.
- Ocarina of Time 3D and Majora's Mask 3D pick bus B's delay from 18 presets for each scene. A rip plays every sound with the preset that the game picks when it starts.
- In one of its modes, WarioWare Gold also puts a pitch shifter on bus A. The rips leave it out.
- `3sfrip` finds a piece of several sequences only by the labels that Mighty Gunvolt Burst gives its sequences. In another game, each of a piece's sequences rips alone.
- The output is stereo.
