3SF 1.5.0 plays the effects that many more games put on the aux buses, including effects that the DSP's firmware runs. It also rips sequences as the game's code starts them: with the tracks that the game quiets, the variables that it sets, and the tempo, pitch, banks and sends that it gives. Later states of a piece rip as files of their own. Rips that use any of this require 3SF 1.5.0. Earlier versions refuse them.

3SF rips Nintendo 3DS sequenced music into a PSF-family format. The rips play with the game's DSP firmware on an emulated Teak DSP. 3SF comes with a ripper (`3sfrip`), a renderer (`3sfplay`), a foobar2000 component and an XMPlay plugin. The format is described in `docs/3sf.md`.

The programs run on Windows 10 or later and require no runtime DLLs. To install the component, open the file with foobar2000 or add it under Preferences › Components. To install the XMPlay plugin, unzip `xmp-3sf-1.5.0.zip` and put `xmp-3sf.dll` in XMPlay's folder, or in a folder inside it. Then restart XMPlay. For macOS, see the README for instructions on building from source.

## New in 1.5.0

- **Effects of more games.** Archive mode now plays the effects of Mario Party: Island Tour, Mario Party: Star Rush, StreetPass Mii Plaza and its add-on games, Luigi's Mansion (2018), six of the 3DS's system titles, Super Mario 3D Land, Animal Crossing: New Leaf and Animal Crossing: Happy Home Designer. The European and Japanese releases of Ocarina of Time 3D and Majora's Mask 3D now get the effects of the North American releases. The README lists each game's effects. `3sfrip` finds Island Tour, Star Rush and the Zelda games by their program IDs. It finds the archives of the Plaza, its add-on games, Luigi's Mansion, the system titles, Super Mario 3D Land, New Leaf and Happy Home Designer by their SHA-1, in a game's RomFS or as loose `.bcsar` files. It prints a note for another build of one of those archives. Rip these games again to hear the effects.
- **The Zelda games' delay.** Ocarina of Time 3D and Majora's Mask 3D pick bus B's delay for each scene. Their rips used to take the preset that the game picks as it starts. They now take the preset that normal play picks most often.
- **Effects in the DSP's firmware.** Some games use effects that the DSP's firmware runs: a delay and a reverb. Archive mode now plays them. Six of the Plaza's add-on games, Luigi's Mansion and Happy Home Designer use them. The Plaza and four of its add-on games link an earlier version of `nw::snd`'s reverb, with another damping filter. Archive mode now plays that version too. `docs/3sf.md` describes the three new kinds of effect.
- **Effect sets.** Some games run an effect for some of their sequences alone. New Leaf puts a reverb on bus A in DJ K.K.'s club and sends his music to it. Happy Home Designer puts a reverb or a delay on bus A by the room's ambient sound. A set can now hold such effects as effect sets, beside the effects of the rest of the archive. The `3sf_aux` tag picks one. DJ K.K.'s music rips with the club's reverb. Each of Happy Home Designer's pieces of music gets two more files, named with `_ringing` and `_echoes`, that play the piece with the room's reverb or delay.
- **Sequences as the game starts them.** A game's code can quiet some tracks of a sequence as it starts, set its variables, set the random numbers, or give it another tempo, pitch, volume, banks or sends. `3sfrip` now rips the listed sequences of Ocarina of Time 3D, WarioWare Gold, Island Tour, Star Rush, New Leaf, Happy Home Designer, Tomodachi Life, Super Mario 3D Land, Mario Kart 7, the Plaza and its add-on games, and three system titles as the game starts them. The new tags `3sf_mute`, `3sf_silence`, `3sf_var_change`, `3sf_seed`, `3sf_tempo`, `3sf_pitch`, `3sf_banks`, `3sf_fx_send` and `3sf_track_volume` hold the start. `3sf_var` now also takes a track's variables. The length analysis plays each sequence with its start. The README describes what each game's rips play.
- **Later states.** Some games move a piece to other groups of tracks, another tempo or other banks as the game goes on. `3sfrip` now writes each listed state as one more file, named after the sequence and the state, such as `SEQ_BGM_REELING_NORMAL_tension_2`. Super Mario 3D Land's voice sequences each get a file with Luigi's voice, named with `_Luigi`.
- **Lengths.** A sequence can repeat its music in a counted loop that runs longer than the length analysis's limit of 600 s. The analysis now measures such a sequence again with its outermost counted loops endless, and gives it two loops and a fade. A tie that only drops the region's volume from a note no longer counts as a change of the sound. The analysis used to count such a pass as a loop, and the sequence's length came out a pass short.
- **Starting playback.** The foobar2000 component and the XMPlay plugin now wait until three seconds of audio are buffered before they start a track, and again after a seek. A busy track then plays through its slow seconds without gaps. A seek into audio that is already buffered, with three seconds after it, is still instant.
- **`--bgm`.** Labels with the words `JNG` or `JG` now mark jingles.
- **Tags in the plugins.** The foobar2000 component keeps every tag that sets how a sound plays when you edit or remove a file's tags, and doesn't show them. Earlier versions kept only `3sf_var`. XMPlay's Message info window leaves those tags out too. The plugins' READMEs list them.
- **Rips that require 1.5.0.** A set whose files hold `3sf_var_change`, `3sf_seed`, `3sf_tempo`, `3sf_pitch`, `3sf_banks`, `3sf_fx_send` or `3sf_track_volume`, or a track variable in `3sf_var`, has bit 4 of its archive descriptor's flags. A set with effect sets has version 3 of the descriptor. A set with the new kinds of effect gives them in the descriptor. 3SF 1.4.0 and earlier refuse such a set. Players before 1.5.0 ignore `3sf_mute` and `3sf_silence`, and play every track. Every other set keeps the layout of 1.4.0.
- **The libraries.** 3SF now builds on ntdlib 3.2.0, with xsflib 1.8.0.

## Features

- **Ripping.** `3sfrip` rips a decrypted 3DS game (`.3ds`, `.cci`, `.cxi` or `.cia`, or an extracted game folder) or a sound archive (`.bcsar`, with a DSP firmware file) into a `.3sflib` and a `.mini3sf` for each sound, or only the music with `--bgm`. A game's rips contain the DSP firmware that the game uses. The firmware can be in a file or in the game's code. `3sfrip` finds the group files that a game keeps outside its sound archive. A piece that a game plays as several sequences gets one `.mini3sf`. `3sfrip` plays each sound to determine its length and fade tags. It also supports drag and drop.
- **Two rip modes.** Game mode runs the game's sound code. Game-mode rips play as the game does. Game mode requires a driver profile for the game. Pokémon X (Japan) has the only one so far. Archive mode plays a sound archive from any game with 3SF's model of the SDK's sound player.
- **Effects.** Game mode plays the reverb that Pokémon X starts in the field. Archive mode plays the effects that the games in its table put on the aux buses, including the DSP firmware's delay and reverb.
- **Starts.** Archive mode rips the listed sequences of known games as the game's code starts them, and their later states as files of their own.
- **Rendering.** `3sfplay` renders rips to WAV or FLAC at the DSP's native 32,728 Hz.
- **foobar2000.** The component plays `.3sf` and `.mini3sf` files using their length and fade tags. It supports tag editing, ReplayGain, endless playback and quick seeking. During playback, the component renders ahead. Seeking within the buffered audio is instant.
- **XMPlay.** The plugin plays `.3sf` and `.mini3sf` files using their length and fade tags. It supports endless playback. Like the component, it renders ahead during playback.
- **Speed.** On 64-bit Windows, the DSP firmware is translated into x86-64 code during playback. The 32-bit component and the XMPlay plugin interpret the firmware. Interpreting is slower. Use the 64-bit component if your foobar2000 and its other components allow it.

## Known limitations

- Seeking ahead of the audio buffer waits for rendering to reach the target. A long seek soon after a track starts can still be slow.
- Game mode only has a profile for Pokémon X (Japan). Other games rip in archive mode. Archive mode's model comes from Pokémon X. A game with another version of the sound library may play differently from the console.
- Only sequences are ripped. Use tools such as vgmstream for BCSTM and AAC streams. Wave sounds are skipped. So are sequences whose data lies outside the sound archive and its group files, or in a group file that `3sfrip` can't find.
- Archive mode plays effects only for the games and archives in its table. Another release or build of them gets none until someone reads its code. A loose `.bcsar` of another game and the other games get none either.
- Some games pick their effects for each scene or stage. A rip plays every sound with the effects that the game picks most often. Ocarina of Time 3D, Majora's Mask 3D, Island Tour, Star Rush and Super Mario 3D Land pick them that way.
- In one of its modes, WarioWare Gold also puts a pitch shifter on bus A. The rips leave it out.
- A game's code can change a sequence while it plays, such as its tempo or the volume of its tracks. A rip plays the start that the game gives the sequence, and the listed later states. Other changes aren't ripped.
- `3sfrip` finds a piece of several sequences only by the labels that Mighty Gunvolt Burst gives its sequences. In another game, each of a piece's sequences rips alone.
- The output is stereo.
